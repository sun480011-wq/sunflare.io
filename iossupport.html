Before you contact us

Please include the following information so we can better understand your request:

  1. Your email address
  2. App name and app version
  3. Device model and iOS version
  4. App Store country
  5. What you need help with
  6. A detailed description of the issue and steps to reproduce it

You can contact us about:

  • App crashes or performance
  • Purchases or subscriptions
  • Account or data issues
  • Ads-related issues
  • Feature requests and other feedback

Please do not include passwords or full payment card details.
